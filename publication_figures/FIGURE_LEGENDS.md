# Figure legends and statistics: SPP1 vs NTC, dura and sorted brain cells

Generated by `make_figures.py`. The script reads the analysis tables in `composition_confounding_analysis/` (dura, run VT3YXB) and `sorted_glia_55ZS67/` (sorted cells, run 55ZS67).

- `png/`: one 300-dpi PNG per claim.
- `stats/`: one CSV per figure with every number behind the asterisks.

## Do these figures correct for multiple comparisons?

Yes, at three levels. Every gene panel prints two of them so a reader can judge.

| Level | Family | Method | Where shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Genome-wide** | all ~19,600 (dura) / ~15,500 (CD11b) tested genes | Benjamini-Hochberg (DESeq2 `padj`) | Printed on every gene panel ("genome-wide padj") |
| **Within figure** | the genes, scores or measures in that figure | Benjamini-Hochberg q | Asterisks |
| **Gene sets** | all sets tested in a model (~66–80) | Benjamini-Hochberg (camera FDR) | "FDR" text on bar charts |

**How to read the two gene-level numbers.** The asterisks answer: *given that we chose to look at these 12 ECM genes, which differ?* That is the standard GraphPad approach (one family per figure). It is only fair when the genes were chosen **before** seeing the data.

Several panels show genes that were picked because they were hits: the robust dura genes, the lipid-arm microglial genes, and partly the ECM panel. For those, the **genome-wide padj is the honest test**:

- **Pass genome-wide (padj < 0.05):**
  - dura: Prg4 (0.010), Cldn11 (0.017), Rgs4 (0.017);
  - microglia: Spp1 (2e-7), Ch25h (0.013).
- **Borderline:** microglia Fabp5 (0.055), Gpnmb (0.099).
- **Do not pass genome-wide:**
  - the dura ECM genes Fn1, Col3a1, Col12a1, Lox, Loxl3, Fbln1, Timp1 and Cthrc1 (padj 0.34–0.69);
  - dura Ifit3b (0.41) and Matn4 (0.67);
  - microglia Apoe (0.16) and Igf1 (0.25).
- **No gene set reaches FDR < 0.05** in either tissue.

**Exact permutation p-values** (stats CSVs) are distribution-free checks:
- Dura (6 v 6, within sex): 400 relabelings, minimum p = 0.005.
- Sorted cells (3 v 3): 20 relabelings, minimum p = 0.1, so **no 3 v 3 comparison can reach p < 0.05 by permutation.**

**Not tested anywhere:** WT vs 5xFAD. WT is drawn as a grey-blue reference only, because genotype is confounded with library processing in the dura run.

Conventions: dots = individual animals (circle = female, triangle = male); line = mean ± SEM; ns q ≥ 0.05, * < 0.05, ** < 0.01, *** < 0.001, **** < 0.0001; FC = fold change, SPP1 / NTC.

## Figures

**00a_workflow_dura.** Dura study design and processing:
- 3 groups × 6 animals (3F/3M);
- whole-dura bulk RNA-seq;
- known limitations carried into the analysis.

**00b_workflow_sorted_cells.** Sorted-cell design: 5xFAD males, 3 per arm; whole-brain warm dissociation; CD11b+ selection; ACSA-2+ selection from the CD11b-depleted fraction. Purity of each fraction, and items still to confirm (sort method, age, SPP1 modality, animal pairing).

**00c_workflow_analysis.** Shared analysis pipeline: QC → composition/purity → DESeq2 (+ adjusted models) → camera gene sets → cross-tissue integration and upload kits.

**01_Spp1_knockdown_dura_and_microglia.** Spp1 mRNA.
- **Dura:** 0.42-fold, DESeq2 p = 0.018. Genome-wide padj 0.67 and permutation p 0.12, so this is not robust: one NTC animal is very high, and the drop is male-driven.
- **CD11b+ microglia:** 0.57-fold, padj 1.9e-7, the top gene genome-wide. All 3 SPP1 samples sit below all 3 NTC samples.
- **Right:** Spp1 relative to the DAM2 program score (Welch p = 0.058; complete separation). The drop is not simply a weaker plaque response.

**02_dura_ECM_genes.** Twelve ECM genes in dura.
- Most matrix genes are lower in 5xFAD SPP1 vs NTC by within-figure correction; Col1a1 and Col1a2 are not.
- Only **Prg4** survives genome-wide correction.
- Read together with figure 03.

**03_dura_ECM_fibroblast_adjustment.** Log2FC ± 95% CI for the same 12 genes, without and with the fibroblast signature as a covariate.
- Most decreases shrink toward 0 and lose significance, so they largely reflect fewer fibroblasts in SPP1 dura.
- Prg4 (down) and Matn4 (up) persist.
- Fbln1 is partly circular: it is a marker in the fibroblast signature.

**04_dura_composition_fibroblast_monocyte.** Fibroblast and monocyte signature scores, plus all 20 composition signatures (SPP1 − NTC, 95% CI).
- Fibroblast is lower (permutation p = 0.025), but **does not survive BH across the 20 signatures (q = 0.50)**.
- Monocyte trends up (p = 0.21).
- So the composition explanation in figure 03 is itself a nominal difference. Fibroblast adjustment is still appropriate as a sensitivity analysis.

**05_dura_robust_SPP1_genes.** Cldn11, Prg4 and Rgs4 are lower in SPP1 dura, genome-wide padj 0.010–0.017. They are significant in 4–5 of 5 covariate models and are the most defensible single-gene dura findings. Ifit3b and Matn4 are up, but only nominally (genome-wide padj 0.41 and 0.67).

**06_dura_pathways_camera.** Top 14 gene sets per model (signed −log10 conservative camera p).
- Unadjusted: matrix / YAP-TAZ / TGF-β / fibroblast-activation sets are down.
- Fibroblast-adjusted: interferon, eosinophil and IL6/JAK-STAT sets are up.
- No set reaches FDR < 0.05 (best FDR 0.59 unadjusted, 0.34 adjusted).

**07_dura_IFN_eosinophil_vs_composition.**
- **Left:** per-animal interferon and eosinophil scores. Permutation p 0.09 and 0.04; BH q 0.09 and 0.08.
- **Right:** the SPP1 effect after adding composition covariates. Adjusting for monocytes removes most of the interferon effect. The eosinophil effect partly survives neutrophil adjustment.

**08_microglia_lipid_arm_vs_core_DAM.**
- **Row 1:** lipid-associated genes fall: Gpnmb, Apoe, Ch25h, Fabp5, Igf1.
- **Row 2:** core DAM genes are unchanged: Cst7, Itgax, Trem2, Tyrobp, Lpl.
- **Row 3:** homeostatic genes are unchanged: P2ry12, Tmem119, Cx3cr1, Sall1, Csf1r.
- Genome-wide: Ch25h padj 0.013; Fabp5 0.055; Gpnmb 0.099; Apoe 0.16.
- Igf1 is BAM-enriched in the reference, so possible carry-over.

**09_microglia_program_scores.** Percent change (95% CI) in 13 published microglial programs.
- All CIs include 0, and all BH q ≥ 0.48.
- The CI widths show what the design could have detected. A change larger than about −15% to +10% in DAM2 is unlikely, for example.

**10_microglia_pathways_camera.** Top 14 gene sets in all genes vs microglia-intrinsic genes only.
- The "up" sets (ECM, angiogenesis, EMT, TGF-β) disappear when carry-over-prone genes are removed.
- Intrinsic sets are all down (MGnD, ARM, lipid, DAM, cholesterol).
- No set reaches FDR < 0.05.

**11_interferon_dura_vs_microglia.** Ten interferon-stimulated genes, log2FC ± 95% CI. Several are up in dura, more so after fibroblast adjustment, but **none changes in brain microglia**. The dura interferon signal is local and tied to cell composition.

**12_cholesterol_SREBP_both_tissues.** Ten SREBP2/cholesterol-synthesis genes in dura (both models), CD11b+ microglia and the ACSA-2+ fraction.
- Effects lean negative in dura (fibroblast-adjusted) and in microglia.
- Only dura Insig1 (fibroblast-adjusted) survives within-figure correction. **No microglial gene is individually significant after correction.**
- The ACSA-2 fraction trends **up**.
- Treat the "shared sterol down-shift" as a weak, two-tissue hypothesis, not a finding.

**13_microglia_sort_carryover.** Non-microglial RNA in each CD11b+ sort: astrocyte marker share, BAM, endothelial, and microglial share.
- Every SPP1 sort exceeds every NTC sort, but at 3 v 3 only the endothelial difference reaches q < 0.05.
- This explains why carry-over genes (Col3a1, Ccn1/2, Pf4, Gpr37l1) rise in SPP1 sorts.

**14_pathways_dura_vs_microglia.** Each dot is a gene set (signed −log10 p).
- Unadjusted, the tissues are weakly anti-correlated (ρ = −0.21), because each tissue's composition artifact runs a different way.
- Fibroblast-adjusted dura vs microglia-intrinsic: ρ = 0.36. Cholesterol/SREBP and mTORC1 lean the same way in both.
- The Spearman p treats overlapping gene sets as independent, so it is optimistic.
