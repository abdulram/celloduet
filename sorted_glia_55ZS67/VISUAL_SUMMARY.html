SPP1 treatment lowers microglial Spp1 without changing the amyloid response

The findings below held up across every robustness check we ran: leave-one-out refits, covariate adjustment, contamination modelling and exact permutation. Weaker or artifact-driven results are listed at the end.

1 · The knockdown works, and it is specific

Each dot is one animal. DESeq2 log2FC −0.81, adjusted p = 2×10⁻⁷. The effect holds when any one animal is dropped and after adjusting for purity, library quality or dissociation (log2FC −0.55 to −1.21).

Not a weaker DAM state. Spp1 is a disease-associated microglia (DAM) gene, but the other DAM genes did not move:

Relative to each animal's own DAM level, Spp1 output is roughly halved (log2 index 0.96–1.68 in NTC vs 0.46–0.70 in SPP1).

2 · No detectable change in 5xFAD pathology programs

We scored published gene sets per animal, with Spp1 removed from every set. All confidence intervals cross zero. DAM intensity scales with plaque exposure, so a large change in plaque load is unlikely. Histology is still needed to confirm this.

SPP1 vs NTC, % change in program score with 95% CI (n = 3 vs 3). The smallest change this design can detect is about ±10–17% for microglial programs and about ±30% for astrocyte and oligodendrocyte programs. The oligodendrocyte interval runs past +30%.

Also unchanged in microglia:

  • MMPs: Mmp14 0%, Mmp2 n.s. Mmp9 and Mmp8 are essentially not expressed.
  • Phagocytic machinery: Trem2, Tyrobp, Mertk, C1q, Cd68, NADPH oxidase.
  • ECM production: microglia-made ECM genes −1% as a group.
  • Spp1 receptors: Cd44 and the integrins (Itgav, Itgb1, Itgb3, Itgb5), in both fractions.

3 · One coherent pattern worth following up

Lipid-processing genes that sit alongside Spp1 in "lipid-laden" macrophages and microglia are lower. Lipid uptake and efflux are not.

There are two possible readings: fewer lipid-loaded microglia, or Spp1 acting upstream of part of the lipid response. Bulk RNA cannot separate them. BODIPY/Plin2 or filipin staining around plaques, or single-cell data, would.

Gpnmb, Ch25h and Fabp5 were first noticed in these same animals, so their changes are not independent confirmation. Apoe (p = 0.002, not significant after correction) and the cholesterol-synthesis set (exact permutation p = 0.2) were checked separately and are only nominal.

Set aside: differences explained by sample handling

  • Most microglia "DE genes" are astrocyte, oligodendrocyte and vascular RNA. SPP1 microglia sorts are slightly less pure (95.5% vs 98.2% microglial RNA).
  • Pseudogene hits track a library-quality axis that differs by arm within each fraction.
  • ACSA-2⁺ differences are mostly cell mix. The fraction is only about 35% astrocyte RNA and 55% oligodendrocyte. An earlier interferon signal vanished per astrocyte.
  • Collagen changes follow fibroblast/vascular carry-over, up in one sort and down in the other.
  • Stress-hormone genes (Fkbp5, Tsc22d3, Sgk1) are driven by one SPP1 animal, so they cannot be separated from harvest stress.
